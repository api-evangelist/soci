Franchise marketing has a scale problem that most automation tools were not built to solve. Managing local SEO for franchise networks with 50, 500, or 5,000 locations requires location-specific content, review management, citation accuracy, and Google Business Profile optimization, all coordinated simultaneously, all with brand governance intact. Most franchise operators know the gap exists.
