For years, social media and search have been treated as separate budgets, separate teams, and separate strategies. That separation doesn’t reflect how Google, or AI platforms, actually work anymore. Social content is showing up inside search results, feeding the signals that decide who ranks, and increasingly, who gets recommended by AI at all.
